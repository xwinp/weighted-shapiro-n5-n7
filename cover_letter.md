# Cover letter — Mathematical Inequalities and Applications

**To:** the Editors, *Mathematical Inequalities and Applications* (mia@ele-math.com)
**From:** Weipeng Xue, Sun Yat-sen University (xuewp5@mail2.sysu.edu.cn)
**Manuscript:** "The Tuan–Thuong weighted Shapiro cyclic inequality for n = 5 and n = 7"

Dear Editors,

I am submitting the above manuscript for consideration in *Mathematical
Inequalities and Applications*. It establishes that the Tuan–Thuong weighted
Shapiro cyclic inequality $P_{n,p,q}(x)\ge n$ holds for $n=5$ throughout
$H_5=(0,1)$ and for $n=7$ throughout $H_7=(0,a_7]\cup[b_7,1)$
($a_7\approx0.21427352091$, $b_7\approx0.32862767792$), with equality only at
the uniform point, by a finite case analysis over the zero-set strata combined
with computer-assisted exact verification.

The principal results include computer-assisted components. The complete proof
artifact, including all exact symbolic certificates, rigorous
interval-arithmetic checkers, machine-readable covering data, and reproduction
instructions, is available at Zenodo (DOI `10.5281/zenodo.21739609`,
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21739609) and at the GitHub release
`v1.0-submission` (https://github.com/xwinp/weighted-shapiro-n5-n7). The artifact is archived
with a SHA-256 manifest and a self-contained git history; the submission
package additionally contains a referee-oriented supplement
(`README_FOR_REFEREES.md`) listing a one-command verification
(`python code/run_all_certificates.py`, 22 certificates) and the load-bearing
exact identities on which review may focus. I would be grateful if these
materials could be made available to the referees together with the manuscript.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
